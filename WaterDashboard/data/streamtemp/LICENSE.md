## Notes

- This is a **derived** dataset. Upstream observations are drawn from
  the Water Survey of Canada (Environment and Climate Change Canada).
  WSC source data is under the
  [Open Government Licence — Canada (OGL-Canada 2.0)](https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada).
  This OBWB-curated dataset adds: long-term banking past WSC's 577-day
  rolling window, project-specific QAQC, daily aggregation,
  and station-metadata harmonisation. The OGL-Canada and CC-BY 4.0
  attribution requirements compose: please credit BOTH OBWB (for the
  curated product) and WSC/ECCC (for the upstream observations) in any
  derived work.
